The idea in one sentence

Diagnose → target → practice → retest.
Don't ask AI to do the learning. Ask it to identify what is weak, teach only what is needed, and then test whether the material can be recalled without help.

This is a trial, not a new homework system. If it doesn't save time or improve retention, stop using it.

Three prompts to copy

1. Diagnostic

Using only the selected course sources, identify the learning objectives for this week's material. Then give me a 10-question diagnostic quiz that tests understanding and problem solving, not simple recognition. Do not show answers until I submit all responses.

2. Target the weak spots

Based on my diagnostic answers, rank my weak areas from most important to least important. Teach only the top weak area first. Use a short explanation, one worked example, then one problem for me to solve without hints. Do not move on until I can solve it correctly.

3. Retest

Create 5 new questions on the concepts I previously missed. Make them different from the earlier questions and slightly harder. Do not reveal answers or hints until I commit to each answer. Afterward, explain the reasoning behind every error.

What not to do

Don't upload the entire internet, spend hours making perfect AI notes, trust old syllabi over the current class, or let AI solve graded work that the professor expects you to do.

Do use AI as a tutor that makes you retrieve, explain, solve, and correct mistakes.

Built as a lightweight experiment for Fall 2026. The goal is better learning with less wasted study time — not more tools.
