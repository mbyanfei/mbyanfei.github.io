Personal Brand Strategy

Victor Fei

Build a durable personal brand around technology, craft, culture and curiosity — with AI and data as the professional core, and photography, Chinese martial arts and travel as the dimensions that make the brand memorable.

Brand Positioning

Recommended positioning:
“I learn complicated technology, build with it, and explain it simply — while exploring photography, martial arts, travel and the craft of continuous learning.”

The goal is not to become another generic “AI influencer.” The stronger brand is a real person with a recognizable point of view: an experienced technologist who builds, explains, photographs, travels, studies traditional disciplines and shares what he learns.

Victor Fei = Technology + Craft + Culture + Curiosity

Distinctive Crossover Topics

  • AI × Photography: generative editing, computational photography, image models and AI-assisted workflows.
  • AI × Martial Arts: pose estimation, movement analysis, training-video feedback and computer vision experiments.
  • Travel × Photography: photo walks, visual essays and “how I shot this” stories.
  • Martial Arts × Learning: deliberate practice, discipline, mastery and what physical training teaches about technical learning.
  • Technology × Traditional Craft: what modern engineering can learn from long-practiced disciplines.

These intersections are more defensible than generic AI commentary because they combine experiences that few creators share in the same way.

Platform Strategy

Home Base

Eventually: victorfei.com or victorfei.ai. Your website should be the durable archive you control.

Authority

LinkedIn + Substack: longer analysis, professional credibility, newsletters and substantive technical content.

Discovery

X + YouTube Shorts + TikTok + Instagram Reels: shorter ideas, visuals, videos and audience growth.

Syndication

Medium + Facebook: secondary distribution rather than separate content operations.

One Idea → Many Assets

1 substantial idea each week

↓ 1 anchor article or visual tutorial

↓ 1 LinkedIn post

↓ 3–5 X posts

↓ 2 short videos

↓ 1 newsletter

↓ Optional Medium repost

Use AI heavily for mechanical transformation, editing, repackaging and production — but keep the core opinion, examples and judgment human. Otherwise the output becomes indistinguishable from AI-generated content spam.

Identity Architecture

Personal / Official

Victor Fei
Use a clean personal email identity for professional, legal and long-term correspondence.

Creator / Public

Victor Fei as the brand, with AI/Data/Technology as subject matter rather than permanently embedding “AI” into the identity.

Family / Private

Keep family-oriented pages and materials separate from public creator infrastructure. Treat ordinary GitHub Pages as public.

Entertainment Experiment

Keep recurring AI-generated baby-and-puppy shorts under a separate entertainment identity so its audience does not dilute the Victor Fei professional brand.

Monetization Path

Do not optimize first for ad revenue or follower count. Build trust and expertise, then monetize higher-value outcomes.

Audience → Newsletter → Reputation → Advisory / Consulting → Guides / Templates → Courses / Workshops → Sponsorships / Affiliates → Products / SaaS

The strategic metric is not “How many followers?” It is “How many people trust Victor Fei enough to read, subscribe, recommend, hire or buy?”

Immediate Priorities

  1. Secure a clean Victor Fei personal email identity.
  2. Secure the best available @victorfei-style handles across major platforms.
  3. Create a separate public GitHub identity for polished public work.
  4. Acquire a durable domain such as victorfei.com and/or victorfei.ai if available and reasonably priced.
  5. Launch actively on only a few channels first: LinkedIn, X, Substack and YouTube/Shorts.
  6. Publish one meaningful idea per week and repurpose it across channels.
  7. Use photography, martial arts and travel to differentiate the brand rather than creating disconnected identities.
  8. Keep family material private and the baby/puppy entertainment experiment separate.
