Smartphone Photography + AI / 手机摄影 + AI

Lesson 1: Composition
第一课：构图

Learn to see before you learn settings. This bilingual lesson uses real-world photography examples to teach rule of thirds, leading lines, natural framing, backlight, and how multiple visual decisions work together.

先学会“看”，再学手机参数。本课用真实摄影案例讲清楚三分法、引导线、框中框、逆光，以及如何让多个视觉元素共同服务于照片。

Core idea / 核心：Think → position yourself → compose → shoot. / 先想 → 找位置 → 构图 → 再按快门。
1. Rule of Thirds / 三分法 2. Leading Lines / 引导线 3. Natural Framing / 框中框 4. Backlight / 逆光 5. Combine Techniques / 综合运用 Practice / 作业

1. Rule of Thirds / 三分法

Rule of thirds

The subject does not have to sit in the center. Give the environment room to participate in the story.

Scale + negative space

A small subject can still dominate attention when placement, line and empty space work together.

English

Imagine two vertical and two horizontal lines dividing the frame into nine sections. The four intersections are naturally strong positions for important subjects.

Do not ask only, “Where should I put the person?” Ask, “How should the person relate to everything else?”

中文

把画面想象成横竖各两条线，分成九宫格。四个交叉点通常都是很有力量的位置。

不要只问“人物放哪里？”更应该问：“人物和周围环境应该形成什么关系？”

2. Leading Lines / 引导线

Road / 道路

Roads, rails, corridors, bridges and shadows naturally guide the eye into the frame.

Perspective / 透视

Lines that converge toward a vanishing point create depth and direction.

English

Your eyes naturally follow lines. A road that begins wide in the foreground and narrows into the distance gives the viewer a visual path.

Foreground → line → destination.

中文

人的眼睛会自然沿着线条移动。让道路从前景进入画面，再向远方收拢，就会形成非常强的纵深感。

前景 → 线条 → 目标。

3. Natural Framing / 天然画框

Frame within a frame

Doorways, arches, windows and tree branches can turn the environment into a visual frame.

Depth / 层次

A strong foreground frame often creates foreground, middle ground and background in a single image.

English

Without a frame, you may simply have “a courtyard.” With a darker arch around it, the viewer receives a much clearer instruction: look here.

  • Foreground — arch
  • Middle ground — courtyard
  • Background — architecture / sky

中文

没有天然画框时，你可能只是拍到“一个院子”。加上较暗的拱门之后，画面会直接告诉观众：看这里。

  • 前景——拱门
  • 中景——院子
  • 背景——建筑 / 天空

4. Backlight / 逆光

Rim light / 轮廓光

Putting the sun behind a subject can create glowing hair, separation and atmosphere.

Golden hour / 黄金时刻

Backlight is especially effective near sunrise or sunset, when the light becomes softer and warmer.

English

A beginner often assumes the sun should illuminate the face directly. Not always. Backlight can create glowing hair, rim light, silhouettes, atmosphere, and better separation from the background.

中文

初学者常认为太阳一定要照在人物脸上。其实不一定。把太阳放到人物后方，可以形成发丝光、轮廓光、剪影、氛围感，并让主体和背景更容易分离。

5. Strong Photos Combine Techniques / 好照片不是只靠一个公式

English

A strong photograph often combines several visual decisions at once:

  • Rule of thirds — the person is away from center.
  • Leading line — the dune ridge directs the eye.
  • Negative space — the open sand and sky create scale.
  • Light and shadow — the terrain becomes more dimensional.
  • Human scale — the tiny person makes the landscape feel enormous.

中文

真正有力量的照片，通常同时运用了多个视觉决定：

  • 三分法——人物没有居中。
  • 引导线——沙丘边缘把视线带向人物。
  • 留白——大片沙地和天空制造空间感。
  • 光影——地形更有立体感。
  • 比例感——小人物让沙漠显得更加巨大。
Photography is not “I used the rule of thirds.” It is multiple visual decisions working together. / 摄影不是“我用了三分法”。而是多个视觉决定共同服务于照片。

Practice Assignment / 第一组练习

Take only 12 intentional photos / 有意识地只拍 12 张：

  • 3 × Rule of thirds / 三分法
  • 3 × Leading lines / 引导线
  • 3 × Natural framing / 框中框
  • 3 × Backlight near sunset / 接近日落时拍逆光

Before each shot ask / 每次拍照前问自己：

  • What is my subject? / 我的主体是什么？
  • What did I deliberately exclude? / 我故意去掉了什么？
  • Why did I choose this angle? / 为什么选择这个角度？
  • Where should the viewer look first? / 我希望观众第一眼看哪里？

Do not shoot 200 random frames and hope one works. Train the decision before the shutter.

不要先拍 200 张再碰运气挑一张。真正要训练的是按快门之前的判断。
