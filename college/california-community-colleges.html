Utah options for international students

Utah has fewer institutions that fit the classic “community college” model. Salt Lake Community College is the state’s institution literally named a community college. Snow College is a public two-year residential college, while Utah State University Eastern is a regional USU campus with several associate-degree options open to F-1 students.

SLCCSalt Lake City area

Salt Lake Community College

Best fit when the goal is a traditional community-college transfer route in Utah, particularly if proximity to the University of Utah matters. SLCC is a commuter college and does not provide on-campus housing.

International application fee: $75 non-refundable.
2026–27 financial proof / estimated annual budget: at least $28,974 in liquid funds. Published budget includes $15,229 nonresident tuition/fees, $9,000 housing/food, $1,700 personal expenses, $900 books/supplies and $2,145 health insurance.
Academic documents: official proof of secondary-school graduation equivalent to a U.S. high-school diploma; English translation may be required; passport biographical page.
English: TOEFL/IELTS is not required for admission. Students complete placement after arrival; students needing additional English are placed in Intensive ESL.
Deadlines from outside the U.S.: Summer Apr 1 · Fall Jun 1 · Spring Nov 1.
Health / visa: F-1 students are enrolled in the college health plan; MMR documentation and TB screening are required before classes.
Transfer: AA/AS/APE programs are designed for university transfer. SLCC reports that 69% of students graduating with one of these transfer degrees move to a four-year institution; among those transfers, the University of Utah is the largest destination in its published quick facts.

SnowEphraim, Utah

Snow College

Public two-year residential college with on-campus housing, an established international office and a clear 2+2 transfer model. It is a strong option when housing, smaller classes and a lower published annual budget matter.

International application: online application, passport, financial statement, academic records and proof of high-school completion; records should be certified and translated into English.
Application fee: the current international admissions page lists $100 non-refundable.
Financial proof: bank statement showing at least $23,000 plus a signed Financial Statement Form.
2026–27 estimated annual cost: $23,000 for two semesters — $15,802 tuition/fees, $1,400 required health insurance and $5,798 living expenses. Snow recommends budgeting another $1,000–$4,000 for travel, visa costs, books and personal expenses.
English for direct academic entry: current admissions materials list IELTS 6.0; TOEFL iBT 4 on the new 1–6 scale (older 63/15 scale accepted through Jan 2027); Duolingo and other tests are also accepted. Snow also offers a conditional ESL pathway.
Important discrepancy: Snow’s current international application page lists Duolingo 110, while the academic catalog still lists 100. Verify the current cutoff with the Center for Global Engagement before applying.
Transfer: an AA/AS is built as the first two years of a bachelor’s degree; Snow states students can transfer to universities throughout Utah, including the University of Utah, if destination transfer requirements are met.
Scholarship timing: Snow publishes international scholarships; spring deadline is Oct 1 and fall deadline is Mar 1.

USU EasternPrice, Utah

Utah State University Eastern

A small regional Utah State University campus with selected associate programs authorized for F-1 students. Especially relevant if the long-term destination may be Utah State University.

Application fee: $50 non-refundable.
Academic records: secondary-school transcripts showing the last three years plus official transcripts from prior colleges/universities. Official records must be sent in sealed form.
English: examples include IELTS Academic 6.0 overall with at least 5.0 in each band, Duolingo 105, or TOEFL iBT 71 on the prior scale; USU lists a 4.0 minimum for TOEFL tests using the new scale after Jan 21, 2026.
2026–27 estimated annual cost: $28,322 for an associate student, including $14,442 tuition/fees plus estimated living and personal costs.
I-20 documents: passport, bank statement / sponsor support, Affidavit of Financial Support, plus visa/I-20 documents when applicable. Proof of finances for one year is required.
Deadlines: Fall priority Mar 1 · Spring priority Oct 1. Summer admission is not available for international students.
Program restriction: only specified programs are authorized for F-1 students; certificates are not available to international students. The General Studies AS/AA is on the approved list.
Class format: international students must take the majority of classes face-to-face to maintain the required physical presence.

Practical selection logic: For a student aiming at the University of Utah, SLCC is the most straightforward Utah community-college-style route because of location and its established transfer volume into U of U, but the lack of campus housing creates a real logistical burden for a new international student. Snow is the cleaner residential/international-student experience and currently publishes the lowest annual budget of these three, with ESL support and on-campus housing. USU Eastern makes the most sense when the student wants a smaller residential setting and may ultimately stay within the Utah State University system.
